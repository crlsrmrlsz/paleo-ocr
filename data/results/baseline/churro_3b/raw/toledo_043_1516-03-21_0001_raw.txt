    Letter from Don Martin de Villagrasa, governor of Brussels, to King Philip II of Spain, dated León, March 21, 1516. The letter discusses the loyalty of the inhabitants and officials of Brussels towards Philip II and the King, mentions a royal privilege related to the city's governance, and requests the king to grant tax exemptions for their livestock.
