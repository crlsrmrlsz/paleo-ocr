To the
Grace and Savoy
King's Lieutenant General
in this Eastern Pyrenees and
Roussillon. The community of the
town of Prats de Molló represents to
you very humbly that Don Diego de
Zuniga our governor with
the most exact collection of the
fifths to which the inhabitants of this
place are subject and the most rigorous
procedures to make them pay have
just now been taken to the point of
seizing the cattle of the poorest
of these inhabitants and of selling
them at a low price and without
any summons or legal procedure.
The supplicants dare not complain
to him for fear of [...]
incurring his displeasure. It is why
they have recourse to you
on the kindness of whom they rely
to find a remedy to such great
evils in the province. They
beg you very humbly my Lord
to deign to order that the said
governor will be admonished to
use henceforth more moderation
and humanity towards the
inhabitants of this place and
to give them the means to
live and to pay the royal taxes
which they have always done
with exactitude and fidelity.
We hope so much from your
justice my Lord [...] your
very humble and very obedient
servants the consul and
inhabitants of Prats de Molló
who pray God to keep you in
his holy grace. Done at Prats
de Molló the 16th of January
1673. The consul and councilors
of Prats de Molló.
